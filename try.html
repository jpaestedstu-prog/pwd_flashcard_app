Try a card, then play a roundSumubok ng card, tapos maglaro

Real cards from the app — the same words, pictures, and example sentences your child or student will learn — and a real round of Word Match, the game every accessibility roster includes. Nothing to install.Totoong cards mula sa app — parehong mga salita, larawan, at halimbawang pangungusap na matututuhan ng inyong anak o mag-aaral — at totoong laro ng Word Match, ang larong nasa bawat roster. Walang i-install.

How it worksPaano ito gumagana

  • 👆Tap the card to flip it and see the word used in a sentence.I-tap ang card para baliktarin at makita ang salita sa isang pangungusap.
  • 🔊Press the sound buttons to hear the word in English or Filipino.Pindutin ang mga sound button para marinig ang salita sa English o Filipino.
  • 🤟Tap 🤟 FSL to watch the word signed in Filipino Sign Language — real clips from the app.I-tap ang 🤟 FSL para mapanood ang salita sa Filipino Sign Language — totoong clips mula sa app.
  • 🎮Switch to Play Word Match for a real round of the app's most-used game — it is in every accessibility roster.Lumipat sa Play Word Match para sa totoong laro ng pinakaginagamit na laro ng app — nasa bawat roster ito.
  • 🎴This demo has 10 words — the full app has 177, plus 15 games, stories, and FSL practice.May 10 salita ang demo na ito — 177 ang nasa buong app, pati 15 laro, kuwento, at FSL practice.
